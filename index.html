Band Mix

Set up at the start of rehearsal. Put the phone in one spot and leave it there.

1. Room and band

2. Mic check

Ear height, away from walls and corners, not dead centre of the room. Have the whole band play at full volume.

3. Soundcheck

Each person plays alone for 30 seconds, as they would in a song. Everyone else stays quiet. Then the whole band plays.

4. The mix
